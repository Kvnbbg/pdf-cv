## Publication rule

Only publish CV versions and supporting material that are intentionally recruiter-facing.

Never place private administrative, tribunal, banking, tax, client or other sensitive records in this public CVthèque. Personal identifiers that are unnecessary for recruitment should be minimized or omitted.

Keep descriptions factual, courteous and professional. Do not fabricate clients, revenue, testimonials, hiring outcomes, certifications or performance metrics.

## Naming convention

Prefer stable, recruiter-readable filenames such as:

`CV-Kevin-Marville-YYYY.pdf`

When a CV is tailored to a role, use a non-sensitive role suffix, for example:

`CV-Kevin-Marville-Developpeur-Web-Mobile-YYYY.pdf`
