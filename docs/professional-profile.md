## Public-repository hygiene

This repository is public and may be reviewed by recruiters. Keep the presentation factual, courteous and professional.

Do not publish or reproduce:

- private administrative, legal, tribunal or financial records;
- personal addresses, phone numbers, identifiers or account information;
- credentials, API keys, tokens, private certificates or secrets;
- client documents or documents containing third-party personal data;
- internal operational details that are not necessary to understand the project.

Public documentation should describe skills, projects and engineering decisions without exposing sensitive personal or third-party information.

## Recruiter-facing principle

A recruiter should be able to understand the profile quickly: **who I am → what I build → where to see it → optional technical depth**.

The tone remains direct, factual and respectful. No fabricated metrics, testimonials, clients, revenue or credentials.
